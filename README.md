## 🇨🇳 中文

跨境电商 Listing 与产品页优化技能，覆盖 Amazon、TEMU、Walmart Marketplace、TikTok Shop、Shopify、AliExpress、Etsy、Google Shopping、Shopee、Lazada、Ozon 等平台与独立站。它把两件紧连的工作合成一个技能：一是 Listing 创建与本地化，把产品事实、供应商标题、截图、竞品页面或中文文案转成平台就绪的标题、五点、描述、A+ 内容、标签、属性、后台搜索词、feed 字段与图片 brief；二是产品优化诊断，排查 PDP 内容、主图、属性、变体、价格展示、优惠券、Buy Box/Featured Offer 就绪度、库存可见问题与被抑制商品。它要求先查官方产品要求、平台政策路径与可用 Seller Center 证据，再给平台相关建议，并强制跑证据门、父子体/多型号门、完整性与搜索词门。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 覆盖 Amazon/TEMU/Walmart/TikTok Shop/Shopify/AliExpress/Etsy 等 12+ 平台
- 供应商标题或中文文案 → 平台就绪标题、五点、描述、A+、后台搜索词
- 关键词与定位地图：核心词、属性词、场景词、受众词、长尾词、风险词
- 强制质量门：每条 claim 记录来源与范围、买家可见文案只用 verified 声明，并按平台现行上限校验完整性
- 父子体/多型号门：不拿单个子 SKU 的尺寸、重量或测试结果锚定共享文案
- 根因诊断：流量、点击、信任、价格、内容、图片、政策、库存、Offer 逐项排查

## 🇬🇧 English

A cross-border ecommerce listing and product-page optimization skill covering Amazon, TEMU, Walmart Marketplace, TikTok Shop, Shopify, AliExpress, Etsy, Google Shopping, Shopee, Lazada, Ozon, and other marketplaces plus independent stores. It merges two tightly connected modes into one skill: listing creation and localization, turning product facts, supplier titles, screenshots, competitor pages, or Chinese copy into platform-ready titles, bullet points, descriptions, A+ content, tags, attributes, backend search terms, feed fields, and image briefs; and product optimization diagnosis across PDP content, main image, attributes, variants, price display, coupons, Buy Box/Featured Offer readiness, inventory-visible issues, and suppressed products. Official product requirements, platform policy paths, and available Seller Center evidence are checked before any platform-specific recommendation, and three mandatory gates run before delivery: evidence/claim, parent-child/multi-model, and listing completeness/search-term.

### ✨ What it does

- Covers 12+ platforms including Amazon, TEMU, Walmart, TikTok Shop, Shopify, AliExpress, Etsy
- Supplier title or Chinese copy to ready titles, bullets, description, A+ content, backend terms
- Keyword and positioning map: core, attribute, use-case, audience, long-tail, and risky terms
- Mandatory quality gates: claims log source and scope, buyer copy uses verified claims only, completeness checked against current limits
- Parent/child/multi-model gate: no single child SKU's dimensions or test results anchor shared copy
- Root-cause diagnosis across traffic, click, trust, price, content, image, policy, inventory, and offer
